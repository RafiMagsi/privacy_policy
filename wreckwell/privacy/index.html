Wreckwell Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-30

Wreckwell is a stress-relief game published by Next Fiction Pvt Ltd. There is no account to create and no server of ours: your progress, moods and journal live on your device.

What stays on your device

Saved locally so the game can remember you. We cannot see it, and it is deleted when you uninstall the app or choose Reset progress in Settings:

  • Scores, stars, unlocked targets, hammers and in-game currency (BONKS)
  • Your optional mood check-ins and any journal notes you write
  • Your settings (sound, music, haptics, screen shake)

Your journal and moods are never uploaded by us. If your phone makes device backups (iCloud or Google backup), those backups may include this app's data, under your own account's rules.

Optional bonus videos (ads)

Wreckwell offers optional rewarded videos in menus, for example "watch a video for bonus BONKS". Ads are never shown while you smash. They are served by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google's SDK may collect your IP address (approximate location), device identifiers (such as the advertising ID on Android), ad and video interactions, and diagnostic data such as launch time and crashes. This data goes to Google, not to us.

  • In the EEA, UK and some other regions, Google's consent form asks for your

    • choice the first time. You can change it any time in Settings > Ad privacy choices.

  • On iPhone we request non-personalized ads and do not ask to track you.
  • On Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID in system settings.
  • Wreckwell Plus removes the videos entirely.

Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Purchases

Wreckwell Plus is a one-time purchase handled by Apple or Google. We never see your payment details. We only learn whether the purchase is active so we can unlock Plus on your device. You can restore it from Settings.

Ratings and reviews

The app may ask you, a few times at most, to rate it using your phone's built-in review prompt. Reviews are handled by the app store, not by us.

Sharing

If you tap Share, Wreckwell hands a short message to your phone's share sheet. You choose where it goes.

Children

Wreckwell is rated 12+ and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Not medical advice

Wreckwell is a playful stress-relief game, not therapy or a medical device. If you are in crisis or in danger, contact your local emergency services.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at the same address with a new date.
